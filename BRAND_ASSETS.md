# Client branding assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using the client JPEG as the logo reference. These are restored interpretations, not a vector tracing. Actual native output sizes (not 4K):

- `public/brand/magix-cloud-tm.png`: 1427 × 1102, transparent PNG. Stationary cloud, TM, Magix lettering and We Mean Clean.
- `public/brand/magix-tools-ring.png`: 1254 × 1254, transparent PNG. Independent animated tools layer.
- `public/brand/home-banner.webp`: 1672 × 941, WebP converted from generated interior photography.
- `design/reference/client-logo.jpeg`: supplied client reference, unmodified.

## Prompt specifications

Cloud: Extract only the central yellow cloud; remove tools and white backdrop; preserve deep blue outline, red handwritten Magix in white oval with red double outline, We Mean Clean and black TM. Transparent background, complete cloud, highest available resolution.

Tools: Separate outer radial tools and starburst rays; preserve tool designs, colors and radial order from the reference; remove cloud, lettering and TM; transparent center and background; clean edges and centered square canvas. Refinement requested removal of fringe and stray pixels while preserving all tools.

Banner: Photorealistic premium household-brand living room, warm ivory walls, pale oak floor, sunlit window with sheer curtains, cream linen sofa and leafy plant on the right, muted blue accent, quiet left half for text, no people, text, logos or cleaning products.

The site adds the two-line caption as HTML for crisp text at every screen size. Animation rotates only the tools; the cloud remains readable and stationary. Pause control and reduced-motion preferences are supported.
